PROJECT 6 
AI-POWERED HOLISTIC STUDENT LEARNING, EMPLOYABILITY AND CAREER PATHWAY RECOMMENDATION SYSTEM 
6.1 Background 
Students often face difficulty in selecting appropriate career paths and determining which skills, courses, projects, certifications, and practical experiences they should pursue. 
Students with different abilities, interests, learning behaviours, personality-related characteristics, and career aspirations are frequently provided with similar learning pathways. 
A student's career readiness is influenced by multiple factors, including: 
Existing technical skills 
Domain and subject knowledge 
Academic background 
Interests and career aspirations 
Learning behaviour 
Willingness to learn 
Communication skills 
Interpersonal relationships and teamwork 
Professional attitude 
Ethical conduct 
Attendance and punctuality 
Cloud Counselage has access to student profiles, learning data, skill information, course participation, project participation, and employment-related information. 
This creates an opportunity to develop an AI-powered system that creates a more holistic understanding of a student's skills, learning behaviour, employability attributes, and career aspirations. 
 
6.2 Problem Statement 
The problem is to develop an AI-Powered Holistic Student Learning, Employability and Career Pathway Recommendation System that analyses multiple dimensions of a student's profile.

6.3 Research Gap 
Existing educational recommendation systems often focus primarily on recommending courses or learning content. 
They may not sufficiently connect the complete student development journey: Student Profile → Skills → Behaviour → Learning → Projects → Employability → Career → Employment 
Many recommendation systems also do not sufficiently consider: 
Technical skills 
Domain knowledge 
Learning behaviour 
Career aspirations 
Employability attributes 
Communication and teamwork 
Willingness to learn 
Practical project experience 
This project will investigate whether an integrated AI-based recommendation system can create a more personalised and explainable pathway from student development to employability and career opportunities. 
 
6.4 Research Objectives 
The project aims to: 
Develop a holistic student profile using academic, skill, learning, behavioural, and career-related data. 
Identify student skill and capability patterns. 
Analyse learning behaviour and engagement. 
Identify technical, domain, and employability skill gaps. 
Classify suitable career pathways and job roles. 
Recommend personalised courses and learning programmes. 
Recommend relevant certifications, projects, and internships. 
Identify areas for employability development. 
Develop an explainable recommendation system that communicates why recommendations are made. 
Evaluate the effectiveness and relevance of the recommendations.
